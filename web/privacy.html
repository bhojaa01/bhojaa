Privacy

Bhojaa helps neighbors share extra food. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it. Last updated 30 October 2026.

What we collect

  • Phone number: to send a login code (OTP) and to connect you with a neighbor after you accept a pickup.
  • Name and email: when you sign in with Google, and to show your name to the neighbor you’re matched with.
  • Address and location: to show food near you and to tell a matched neighbor where to collect. Your exact house number is hidden until you accept.
  • What you post: food listings, food requests, pickups, ratings, reports and help & support chats.
  • Basic visit data: pages opened, device type and approximate area, to understand how Bhojaa is used.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your data or use it for ads. Only the neighbor you’re matched with sees your name, phone number and pickup address. We use a few services to run Bhojaa: Google (sign-in), a WhatsApp messaging provider (to deliver login codes), a map service (to turn a location into an area name), and our hosting and database providers. They process data only to provide these services.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Delete your account

You can delete your account anytime from Account → Delete my account in the app. This permanently removes your profile and everything linked to it. If you can’t log in, see how to request deletion.

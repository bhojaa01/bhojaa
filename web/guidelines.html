Community guidelines & safety

Bhojaa is for neighbors sharing extra food. Pickup only. Free.

Be kind and honest

Describe what’s in the food, including allergens. Don’t list anything unsafe or expired beyond what you’d eat yourself.

Pickup, not delivery

The collector comes to you. Share the house number only after you accept. Meet at the gate if you prefer.

No payment

Don’t charge. Don’t sell. This is leftover food, not a store.

Safety

If something feels wrong, decline. Report a problem from the app or email hello@bhojaa.app.

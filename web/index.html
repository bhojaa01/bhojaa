What Bhojaa does

Bhojaa connects neighbors. If you have extra food, post it. If you need a meal, ask nearby. The giver accepts, then you collect from their home. No delivery. No payment. Address stays private until they accept.

Common questions

Is the food safe?

Bhojaa is neighbor-to-neighbor. Use your judgement, check the note for allergens, and collect only food you are comfortable eating. We do not inspect or certify meals.

Can I give packed food?

Yes. Leftover homemade food, packed meals, fruit, and similar extras are welcome. Say what’s inside and any allergens in the note.

What if nobody’s nearby?

Listings are shown only within a short pickup radius. If no one is close, wait a little or try again later. Pickup is always free and local.

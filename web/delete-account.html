Delete your Bhojaa account

Your account has been deleted. Your profile and everything linked to it have been removed. Thank you for sharing food with your neighbors.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Bhojaa and log in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Can’t log in?

Email bhojaa01@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Bhojaa account” from the email address you sign in with, or include the phone number you use with Bhojaa. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, email, address and location
  • Food you listed and food requests you posted
  • Your pickups, ratings and reviews
  • Your help & support chats and reports you sent
  • Login codes (OTPs) linked to your number

Deleted data can’t be recovered. We don’t sell or share your data.
